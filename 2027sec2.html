Frequently Asked Questions

The lessons are suitable for G2 students because they learn similar content as G3 students. Schools may also assess G2 students at the G3 level based on the school's preference and student profile.

The lessons are only suitable for IP students who learn mainstream topics. Please note that questions in the lessons are set according to mainstream assessment specifications, which might differ from IP.
Content lessons do not have a fixed cap. We will close registration once we reach optimal capacity. This ensures that every cohort remains at a size that allows for collaborative learning and meaningful teacher-student interaction.

Exam prep lessons do not have a cap because lessons are conducted in a seminar-style with Q&A. These lessons are comprehensive walkthroughs that focus on mastering answering techniques and deconstructing exam questions.
Pre-class materials (i.e. notes & mocks) and the Zoom link are sent at least 1 week before the lesson. Recordings and any supplementary materials are sent within 3 business days after the lesson.
Simply email us to submit your mocks for marking or to ask questions. We will review and respond within 3 business days (excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and Public Holidays).
Reach out to the Krunch team at keepitkrunchy@gmail.com. They’d be happy to support you!

About Our Instructors

Geography – Ms Stacy

@geographer.stace

Ms Stacy has been teaching Geography for more than 10 years. She formerly taught at Balestier Hill Secondary and Beatty Secondary. She also spent time at MOE’s Curriculum Planning and Development Division, where she was involved in developing learning materials and training teachers in implementing the Geography syllabus. She is a published author with EPH, writing the SEC G3 Pure & Elective Geography Ten-Year Series in her name, and the Geography Fast Forward Series under her pen name “Shermaine Wee”.

History – Mr Mike

@mrmike.history.ss

Mr Mike is a seasoned History, Social Studies and Geography educator with 15+ years of teaching experience across the SEC and Integrated Programme tracks. His career includes tenures at St. Patrick’s School, Serangoon Secondary and CHIJ St. Nicholas' Girls School. He also spent time at MOE’s Curriculum Planning and Development Division, where he was involved in developing and training teachers in implementing the Social Studies syllabus.
